A few useful answers

Before we get started.

How long does a website take?

Timing depends on the number of pages, content readiness and requested features. A realistic timeline is agreed after the project scope is confirmed.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can review the current structure, identify what should be kept and plan a redesign around your business goals.

Are domain and hosting included?

Unless specifically stated in your proposal, domain registration and hosting are separate third-party costs.

Will the domain and website be mine?

Yes — completely. Your domain is registered in your own name and the website is your property. We never hold a client's site hostage; that is confirmed in writing before work begins.

How do I start a project?

Send a short message using the form below or contact us on WhatsApp. We will discuss your goals, scope and next steps.
