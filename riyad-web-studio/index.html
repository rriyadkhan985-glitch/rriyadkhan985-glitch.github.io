Case Studies

How each project was thought through

Good websites are decisions, not decorations. Two of our demos, broken down.

Meridian
Builders

Meridian Builders — construction company website

ConstructionConcept Demo

Objective

Developers sell flats from Facebook posts that vanish in two days. The goal: one permanent link where a buyer — often an expatriate Bangladeshi — can study projects seriously and book a visit.

Design decisions

Dark, confident editorial layout with a single strong accent; project cards carry real starting prices so nothing feels hidden; every screen pushes toward one action — book a site visit.

Key features

  • Interactive 3D building viewer
  • Filter projects by budget & area
  • Flat cost calculator and live construction progress tracker
  • Visit booking that lands on WhatsApp
QR
Ordering

Restaurant QR Ordering — scan, order, done

RestaurantConcept Demo

Objective

Busy restaurants lose orders at peak hour and printed menus are costly to change. The goal: guests order from their own phone in under a minute, and the owner can change the menu anytime.

Design decisions

Built phone-first for one-hand use at a table; big photos, bigger buttons; no download, no sign-up — the two things that kill QR systems. The owner gets an admin view of incoming orders.

Key features

  • Table QR code opens the live menu instantly
  • Cart and order placement from the guest's phone
  • Orders reach the restaurant immediately
  • Menu and prices editable without printing anything
